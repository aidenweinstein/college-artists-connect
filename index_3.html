Our story

In 2025, five sophomores at the University of Texas at Austin founded Longhorn Artists Connect — the first-ever student-run talent agency. Within a year, students at other schools started chapters of their own, and College Artists Connect was born.

Want to start a chapter at your school? Reach out and we'll help you get going.
